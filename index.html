Who is Archi-talk for?

Archi-Talk brings together architects and designers, students and young professionals, researchers and universities, industries and manufacturers, institutions and decision-makers, entrepreneurs, developers, investors and media. Why come? To discover, learn, experience, connect, identify opportunities and build collaborations in one ecosystem.

ARCHITECTURE x RESEARCH x INDUSTRY x TECHNOLOGY x EDUCATION x INSTITUTIONS

Why is Archi-talk different?

Many events talk, exhibit, teach, sell or network. Archi-Talk connects these dimensions and turns them into a continuous journey: TALK -> LEARN -> TEST -> DOCUMENT -> CONNECT -> BUILD Its difference is the combination of a 10-edition legacy, a cross-disciplinary ecosystem and a stronger culture of experimentation and real-world application.
